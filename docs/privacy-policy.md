# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

RevCollect is a collections workspace. This policy explains what we store when you use the website and the app, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

## Information we collect

Account information: your name, email address, and sign-in details.

Billing information: the subscription record for the workspace. Card details are handled by the payment processor. We store the customer and subscription identifiers needed to keep the plan active.

Workspace information: settings you save, and the collection emails stored for the workspace.

Ledger information: when you connect Xero, QuickBooks Online, or Zoho Books, we store the company connection, customers, invoices, and payments needed to show open receivables.

Mail: we send mail only through the Gmail account you authorize. We store the collection emails sent from the workspace.

Usage information: IP address, device, and browser data used to operate and secure the service.

## How we use it

We use this information to show your inbox, draft follow-ups that you review, and record what you sent. AI drafts are inference only. We do not use Xero, Intuit, or Zoho API data to train or fine-tune machine learning models.

We do not sell customer lists.

## How long we keep QuickBooks data

We keep the QuickBooks snapshot (the company connection, customers, invoices, and payments) and the collection emails stored for the workspace for as long as the workspace exists.

Disconnect from QuickBooks revokes our token and stops new syncs. The last snapshot stays so you can still read it. Disconnecting Xero or Zoho Books from Settings does the same for that ledger.

## How to delete it

Sign in and choose Settings → Privacy and data → Request data deletion. That revokes the QuickBooks, Xero, Zoho, and Gmail tokens and deletes the stored books, payments, and sent collection mail. The subscription record stays.

You can also email admin@revcollect.ai. Requests sent by email are completed within 30 days.

## Sharing

We share information with the providers that run the service: hosting, database, email (Gmail), the accounting system you connect, payments, and inference for drafts. We disclose information if the law requires it, or if the business is transferred.

## Your choices

You can disconnect a ledger in Settings. You can ask for access, correction, or deletion as described above. You can opt out of marketing email by using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.
